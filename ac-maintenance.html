Maintenance · South Florida

Maintenance that
actually pays off.

In South Florida a system runs nearly year-round. Twice-yearly service is not upselling; it is what keeps a ten-year unit from becoming a six-year unit.

Book onlineCall (786) 372-7744

What we check

Full system inspection, outdoor coil cleaning, refrigerant levels, electrical connections, drain lines, filter replacement, and thermostat calibration.

Why twice a year here

Our humidity and cooling load put far more hours on equipment than in most of the country. Annual-only service tends to shorten equipment life.

Membership option

Our Deep Freeze plan bundles two tune-ups, priority dispatch, discounted repairs and free diagnostics into a flat monthly rate.

Catch problems early

A failing capacitor found in March is a small part. The same capacitor found in August is an emergency call during a heat wave.
