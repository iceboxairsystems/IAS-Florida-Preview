How billing works

  • Written estimate before any work begins; you approve the price first
  • Estimates and diagnostic visits are $99, waived when you approve the work and we complete it
  • Outside regular business hours (Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM), a $175 after-hours fee is added, quoted by phone before we dispatch
  • If the scope changes mid-job, we stop and get your approval
  • No payment collected before work is completed on standard service calls
  • Larger installations may require a deposit, stated in writing on the estimate
