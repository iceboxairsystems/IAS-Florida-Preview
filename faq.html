Service and scheduling

For emergencies, we dispatch every day until 10 PM, and same day we will fix it, better and cleaner, across Miami-Dade and Broward. For non-urgent work we offer same-day or next-day appointments. Pick a time here.
Our regular business hours are Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. The phone line is open 24/7, and we dispatch emergencies every day until 10 PM; outside regular hours a $175 after-hours fee applies.
Call anyway. Our line is open 24/7: it takes your details and we book you for the first appointment the next morning. If you smell burning or see smoke or sparks, turn the system off at the breaker, and if anyone is in danger, call 911 first.
All of Miami-Dade and Broward, residential and commercial. That includes Homestead, Kendall, Coral Gables, Brickell, Miami Beach and Sunny Isles, up through Hollywood, Fort Lauderdale, Weston and Parkland.

Twice a year is the standard recommendation for South Florida. Our humidity and near year-round cooling load put far more hours on a system than in most of the country, so annual-only service tends to shorten equipment life. That's why our Deep Freeze membership includes two visits.

Pricing and payment

Estimates and diagnostic visits are $99. If you approve the work and we complete it, the $99 is waived, and Deep Freeze members never pay it. Either way, you get the price in writing before any work begins. Request your estimate.
Yes. Service outside regular business hours (weekdays after 5 PM, weekends and holidays) adds a $175 after-hours fee to the visit, quoted on the phone before a technician is sent, never after. If it can wait, we can book the next business day instead at no extra charge. Deep Freeze members don't pay the fee for emergencies (no cooling, a water leak or an electrical hazard) once their membership has been active for 30 days.
Yes. We offer flexible payment options on new system installations. Your technician walks you through a short application on-site and most decisions come back during the same visit. See how financing works.
Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover, plus cash and check. Financing is available on new system installations. Autopay is optional: set it up here.
On standard service calls, after the work is done, never before. You approve a written estimate first, and if the scope changes mid-job we stop and get your approval. Larger installations may require a deposit, stated in writing on the estimate.
Yes. Open our customer portal and enter the email or phone number on your account; we send you a secure sign-in link. From there you can view and pay invoices, review and approve estimates, and see your upcoming and past visits.
Yes, anytime, with no contract and no cancellation fee. Use our online cancellation form, call (786) 372-7744 or email jobs@iasflorida.com, and we confirm in writing within one business day.
